About Us

A Foundation Built on Compassion

InAmigos Foundation is an NGO based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, incorporated on 23rd September 2020 under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. Since our founding, we have worked tirelessly for the betterment of society — uniting minds, hearts and hands for meaningful change.

Our Vision

To create a compassionate and inclusive society where every individual has access to education, nutrition, healthcare and dignity — a world where no one is left behind.

What We Do

Our Projects

Six focused initiatives, each built to serve a specific need in our community.

🌳

Project Prakriti

20,000+ Trees Planted

Environmental restoration through mass plantation drives across Chhattisgarh.

🍛

Project Seva

50,000+ Meals Distributed

Daily hot meals delivered to slum areas, orphanages, and festival & community kitchens.

✨

Project Udaan

900+ Girls Empowered

Digital campaigns empowering girls through education and skill development.

🐾

Project Jeev

50+ Animals Fed Daily

Street animal feeding drives paired with veterinary welfare support.

📚

Project BachpanShala

100s of Children Educated

Free education for rural children, including books, stationery and mentoring.

💡

Project Vikas

Skill Development

Promoting skill development, creativity and innovation through practical learning opportunities.

Beyond Our Projects

Our Social Work

Everyday efforts that support communities alongside our six core projects.

🍲

Food Drives

Daily meals in slum areas and orphanages, along with community kitchens during festivals and emergencies.

🤲

Social Work

Sponsoring education for underprivileged children and organising vocational training for youth and women.

🎁

Charity Drives

Winter clothes and blankets for the homeless, along with school supplies for students in need.

🧹

Cleanliness Drives

Street cleaning in urban and rural areas, and installing dustbins and waste segregation points.
